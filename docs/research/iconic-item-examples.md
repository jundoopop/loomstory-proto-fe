## Authoring and media rules

- Separate original design history, the pictured product edition and each contextual reference.
- Campaign participation documents a campaign association. It does not authenticate an uploaded object or establish personal ownership.
- Keep credited source pages and natural photograph dimensions with media selected later. No photographs were downloaded or added to Git for this task.
- Keep the existing resale block disabled. No live prices, inventory or resale estimates were collected.
- The proposed dossier wording below paraphrases the primary records. Choose the corresponding edition before supplying its photo or measured diagram coordinates.
- Existing UI metadata can represent campaigns as Editorial; use same-design only for an explicitly documented design-family association. Use On screen for verified screen references and preserve version distinctions in the description.
- Brand ambassadorship alone is insufficient to identify a particular worn item. A related boot, collaboration or reissue needs its own edition caption.
