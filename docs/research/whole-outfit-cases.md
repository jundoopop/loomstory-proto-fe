# Celebrity whole-outfit examples

Researched: 5 November 2026. The two PUMA examples are now integrated in FirstSight; the other
cases remain research leads. See the [implementation ledger](../plans/whole-outfit-example-plan.md).

The shipped results use published real model names and product photographs, with the frame
association explicitly labelled as curated. No exact worn-SKU confirmation or live detection is
claimed. Eight source photographs are restored locally into ignored media storage.

Related: [implementation proposal](../plans/whole-outfit-example-plan.md) and the
[broader iconic-item research](iconic-item-examples.md).

## User's inclusion rule

Only documented real brand/model identities appear in results. A photograph can contain other
visible clothes without labelling or selling them as identified pieces. All eligible pieces appear
together; the featured bag is initially highlighted. Exact retail codes are optional when a named
model family is documented, but must never be inferred from visual resemblance or historical price.

## Candidate evidence

### 1. ROSÉ × PUMA, August 2025 — strongest garment-and-bag candidate

The [PUMA Japan release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000290.000010697.html) supplies
a campaign frame and named product packshots:

| Visible candidate | Published reference |
| --- | --- |
| Micro Grip bag | 091927 |
| Slim Longsleeve Tee | 633805 |
| Slim Micro Mini Skirt WV | 633798 |

The [selected campaign frame](https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/10697/290/10697-290-4bc42be6f6e2348cfb62cf1241e3be24-1430x614.jpg)
shows a black handbag, white long-sleeved top and black miniskirt. The three same-release packshots
were visually inspected and match their design features. This is a source-supported authoring
candidate, **not an explicitly captioned frame-to-SKU wear list**. Confirm that linkage before
describing the worn items as exact references. No footwear is visible in this frame.

The collection also contains named T7 trousers, but collection membership alone does not put those
trousers in this photograph. Do not attach them to this example.

A separate [seated campaign frame](https://pmh-production-public-bucket.nbg1.your-objectstorage.com/articles/2025/a-rose-for-a-rose-roses-first-puma-collection-is-a-personal-tribute/25aw-sp-rose-collection-look1-0336-rgb_0.jpg)
from the [global release](https://mediahub.puma.com/articles/a-rose-for-a-rose-roses-first-puma-collection-is-a-personal-tribute)
provides a trousers-first candidate: Relaxed Mesh Top 633804_01, T7 Relaxed Track Pants WV
633796_01 and Speedcat PRM 404391_01. These match the corresponding official packshots; the same
uncaptioned frame-to-SKU limitation applies. No bag belongs to that selected frame.

### 2. Ren Meguro × Fendi, Narita, 22 September 2025 — named bag and trainers ready

The [brand-issued release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000557.000003876.html) identifies
Flux Messenger and Fendi Force. Both are visible in the
[inspected portrait](https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/3876/557/3876-557-a6ded0516e0ed6c1af58ea0334c60df0-2025x2700.jpg).

The T-shirt, hoodie and grey jeans are documented as garments, but the release does not name
exact models for them. Exclude those under the user's strict model rule. The miniature Baguette
is part of a charm, not a second full-size bag. Historical release prices are not current resale data.

### 3. Song Hye-kyo × Fendi, Seoul, Winter 2023 — Korean bag-and-boots alternative

The [brand-issued release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000365.000003876.html) names
Peekaboo ISeeU Petite and Fendi First boots. The left standing frame of its
[three-panel image](https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/3876/365/3876-365-c49bcf82d246ab575fff30aad653774d-1280x720.jpg)
shows both. The jacket/skirt lack exact model names and are excluded by the selected rule.

The right frame contains a different silver Baguette. Do not merge both bags into one outfit or
detect display bags on the shop shelves. A single-frame source is preferable for photo selection.

### 4. ROSÉ × Levi's, February 2026 — named trousers-and-jacket research lead

The [official campaign release](https://www.levistrauss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PressRelease_BehindEveryOriginal_Campaign.pdf)
explicitly associates ROSÉ with Loose Boot cut jeans and a Relaxed Fit Trucker.
The [campaign page](https://www.levistrauss.com/2026/02/08/levis-behind-every-original/)
contains a credited ROSÉ photograph. Downloading that still returned HTTP 403 during this research;
the exact same-frame visibility has not been verified. Keep it as a research lead rather than a
ready whole-outfit sample.

## Recommendation and readiness gate

The standing and seated PUMA examples implement the garment/bag and trousers journeys through
clearly labelled curated associations. Their exact worn-SKU mapping remains unconfirmed and must
not be promoted to an exact-match claim. Keep the two Fendi cases as further named-model pairs.
Continue the Levi's lead for a trousers example. Do not promise that every visible piece is known.

Inspection copies are temporary files outside the repository. Existing ignored-media setup remains
the future integration path. Source credit, publication permissions and exact identity evidence are
separate metadata; none of these records establish a real seller, authentication or live inventory.

The research did not collect live prices, stock or valuations. Implementation separately adds
six illustrative archive-study listings; their sample commerce assumptions are not source facts.
