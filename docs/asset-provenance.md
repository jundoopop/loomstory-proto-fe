# Prototype asset provenance

The garment contact sheet was created with the built-in image generation tool on 3 November 2026. It depicts six fictional, unbranded garments and is not a photograph of real inventory. `public/images/garments.png` retains the output; `garments.webp` is its optimized web encoding. Cards select cells with CSS, without asserting exact real-product matches.

Prompt: “Use case: product-mockup. Create a polished photographic contact sheet for a fictional vintage clothing archive website. Landscape image, precisely three equal-width columns and two equal-height rows, six equal rectangular cells, edge to edge, no margins, no gutters. Each cell contains exactly one complete garment in beautiful soft directional studio lighting, flat lay front facing, garment centered with breathing room, all edges visible. Consistent warm light beige limestone paper backdrop #e8e2d6 in every cell. Top left: aged dark chocolate brown leather bomber jacket with classic collar and zip, worn patina. Top middle: long camel belted trench coat with collar and dark buttons. Top right: vintage faded mid-blue straight-leg jeans. Bottom left: charcoal wool oversized tailored blazer. Bottom middle: rich burgundy cable-knit crewneck sweater. Bottom right: black A-line 1960s-inspired minidress with cream pointed collar. High end archival fashion e-commerce photography, tactile fabric grain, softly sculpted folds, gentle shadows, restrained palette, beautifully convincing materials. NO text, NO labels, NO branding, NO logos, NO humans, NO hangers. All garments are fictional unbranded sample designs. The exact grid placement is essential, each garment occupies one cell. Deliver one image.”

Only original summaries and links are reused from the six cited museum/cultural pages; their photographs are not republished. Each reference’s publisher, URL, locator, and access date are in `lib/catalogue.ts` and visible on the story/detail screen. Context does not imply an identification or provenance link.

DM Sans and Libre Caslon Display are self-hosted from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. License files are retained in `public/fonts/`.
